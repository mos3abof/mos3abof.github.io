Zobad.net

A daily digest of the best of the internet for Arab users: videos, articles, news, and polls, curated for quality and relevance.

2011

A platform that aggregates the best content on the internet for Arab users. Every day it surfaces a curated selection of videos, photos, posts, news, and polls. The defining criterion is quality and usefulness rather than virality. Users could also submit and vote on content through an interactive section separate from the editorial picks.
