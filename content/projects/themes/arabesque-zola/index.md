A theme for the static site generator [Zola](https://www.getzola.org) that brings the
[Arabesque design system](@/projects/arabesque-design-system/index.md) to blogs and
websites: calm, accessible, and bidirectional, in English and Arabic, light and dark.
It covers every template a site needs, from articles and tags to an archive and search,
along with the design system's components and a set of shortcodes, with no tracking
and nothing loaded from elsewhere.
