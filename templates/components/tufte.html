{#- Tufte-style components, for pages with extra.tufte = true (templates/page.html, styled in css/tufte.css). After https://rstudio.github.io/tufte/ and Tufte CSS. On another page they still work, in the text: notes in a smaller type, after their reference. Usage: Text.{{ }} Text.{{ }} {{ }}, Tufte starts a section so. {% %}The quote.{% %} {% %}Markdown that takes the text's width and the margin's.{% %} sidenote, marginnote text (string) The note, in Markdown (inline: links, emphasis, code, images). In the margin beside the line, on the right in English and on the left in Arabic. On a narrow screen, hidden behind its number (sidenote) or ⊕ (marginnote), which shows it under the line. Put no space before it: the number sits on the word, as a footnote's does. Its output is on one line, so the paragraph it's in isn't broken. newthought text (string) The first words of a section, in small capitals. epigraph author (string, optional) Who said it. source (string, optional) Where: a book, a talk. In italics, after author. Body: the quote, in Markdown. fullwidth Body: Markdown (a table, a figure, a wide code block) across the text and the margin.
